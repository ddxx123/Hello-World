1) Quick Lesson: Anatomical Position

  • Body is standing upright and facing forward.
  • Feet are shoulder-width apart, parallel, and toes point forward.
  • Upper limbs are at the sides and palms face forward.
  • Terms are described as if the body is in this standard position to reduce confusion.
  • Prone = face down; supine = face up.
